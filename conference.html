What I Have Learned

In the October 2026 General Conference, I learned about the importance of having faith in Jesus Christ. I learned that when I have faith, I can trust that Heavenly Father will help me through my challenges.

I also learned that I can have courage when I trust in Jesus Christ. I can be brave and stand up for what I believe, even when it is hard. I learned that I can be a disciple of Jesus Christ by following His teachings.

General Conference reminds me of the importance of staying close to Jesus Christ because He is the center of our lives and the source of our strength. Trusting in Him helps me navigate through life's challenges.

My Favorite Speakers

1. President Dallin H. Oaks

President Oaks taught about that we need to be patient with one another even ourselves. He reminded me that when I have questions or problems, I can get closer to Jesus Christ.

I love his message because it reminds me that we can always turn to Jesus Christ for help and guidance. He taught that protecting ourselves and others is to renewing our covenants by partaking of the sacrament with consistent effort.

What I learned: He invited me to act on the truths and share them with others that we may all draw closer to Jesus Christ our Savior.

2. President Henry B. Eyring

President Eyring taught that God knows our heart and that he sent faithful disciple to help Him keep His promise to succor others in their distress.

I love listening to his message because it reminds me to fear not because God is with me and that He will help me through my challenges and that I can have faith in Him.

What I learned: IThank those who have responded to the call to be the Lord's servant to fulfill His promise to His children.

3. Elder Neil L. Andersen

President Andersen taught about having spiritual courage. He taught that being His true disciple means overcoming the world and keeping His commandments and trusting in Him and His promise.

I liked his message because it reminded me that if we choose faith in Christ, He blesses us with the courage to do what is right, even when things are hard.

What I learned: I can have courage when I trust Jesus Christ and try to follow His teachings.

Testimony

I am grateful for the teachings of the leaders of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints. I know that they are called of God to guide us

I want to continue to learn from their teachings and apply them in my life. I know that as I do so, I will be blessed with greater faith, courage, and strength to face the challenges of life. I am willing to follow the teachings of Jesus Christ and strive to be a better disciple of Him.
